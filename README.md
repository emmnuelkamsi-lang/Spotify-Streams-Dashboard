# Spotify Streams Analysis

Analysis of the most-streamed Spotify tracks: how danceable and energetic they are, when they were released, and whether they are in a major or minor key.

## Preview

## Key numbers

- Tracks: 952
- Total streams: 489bn
- Average danceability: 69.55%
- Average energy: 66.22%
- Average acousticness: 24.14%

## Key findings

- **Popular tracks are danceable and energetic.** Average danceability is 69.55% and energy is 66.22%, while acousticness is low (24.14%). The most-streamed tracks lean toward upbeat, produced sounds rather than acoustic ones.
- **Major keys lead, but not by much.** 55% of tracks are in a major key and 45% are in a minor key.
- **Recent releases dominate.** Most streams come from tracks released in recent years (2021 to 2023), and streams drop off sharply for older releases.
